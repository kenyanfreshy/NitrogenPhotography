The studio

Capturing priceless moments

Nitrogen Studios is a photography and videography studio in Salgaa, along the Salgaa–Elburgon road. We work in our own studio and on location, and bring drone and livestream kit to the events that need them.

The work is known for bold colour, flattering light and clean, vivid finishing. In 2025 the studio was named Best Photography of the Year at the Kitenge Fashion Fest & Awards.

Karibu sana. We value you.
